Year in Review and a Look Ahead

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

Draft — Compliance Review Required

Genuinely useful real company-history content (the Failla Financial → Strada rebrand, the 2012 fee-only/fiduciary registration change, the TD Ameritrade custodian switch) — but the fiduciary/registration claims still need the standard compliance pass before republication.

Each winter, I spend a lot of time doing what we call practice management. Many things have happened this last year — we updated our business name, rebranded the firm, launched blogging and social media campaigns, changed registrations, created a new website, and changed custodian firms.

Rebranded firm name

Our original business name, Failla Financial, was a great name — one that fit when I left Merrill Lynch in 2008. But there is an entire team I work with, and I wanted a firm name that represents our entire unit. Strada in Italian literally means "street," but figuratively it means path, way, or direction — as I review each client's financial and personal life, we are metaphorically proceeding down that path together as a team.

Firm registration changes

Back in 2012, I started to practice fee-only financial advice, but until I changed the registrations of the firm, I could not use the word "fiduciary." My fiduciary standard is to you, not the broker-dealer and not the insurance firm.

Custodian changes

I reviewed a lot of custodians this past year and chose TD Ameritrade — for ease of use for my clients, reduced fees on trades, and the institutional capability to hold alternative investments we utilize to reduce market exposure.

Electronic signature and reporting

We are instituting DocuSign so clients can sign forms electronically. Since re-registering the firm, we are also working on a Q1/Q2 initiative for complete data aggregation of all investments onto one client statement and website login.
