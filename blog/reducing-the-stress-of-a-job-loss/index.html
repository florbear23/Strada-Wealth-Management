Reducing the Stress of a Job Loss

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

Job loss can happen fast and be completely unexpected. Listen to your exit interview, take your paperwork, and go home — do not sign anything until you've had a chance to review it.

Health insurance: COBRA lets you continue coverage at the group rate (typically 100% of the premium plus a 2% admin fee); healthcare.gov may offer less expensive independent or short-term coverage depending on your situation.

Budget: sit down and list every known expense. You cannot ignore your bills, but you can call and ask for a payment plan or extension.

Get organized: treat the job search like a full-time job. Get support: talk to friends, stay occupied, and know that you can succeed in another role.
