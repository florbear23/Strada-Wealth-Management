In-Depth Guide to Retirement Savings: Understanding IRAs and 401(k) Plans

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

Navigating retirement savings plans is vital for a secure financial future. This guide covers Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs) and 401(k) plans.

401(k) plans

A Traditional 401(k) uses pre-tax contributions; a Roth 401(k) uses post-tax contributions that grow and withdraw tax-free under qualifying conditions. Employer matching is effectively free money. Early withdrawals attract a 10% penalty and are taxed as ordinary income.

IRAs

A Traditional IRA may allow tax-deductible contributions with taxable withdrawals in retirement; a Roth IRA is taxed upfront but yields tax-free qualifying withdrawals.

Opening an IRA

Select a custodian based on fees, investment options, and service; choose the right IRA type for your goals; and select investments aligned with your risk tolerance and timeline.
