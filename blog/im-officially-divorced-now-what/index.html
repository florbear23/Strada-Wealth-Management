I'm Officially Divorced. Now What?

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

Once a divorce decree is finalized, clients often ask, "What now?" Once it's filed, the relationship with the divorce attorney typically ends unless modifications are needed — but your work is just beginning.

We help clients establish what needs immediate attention: portfolio management, cash management, tax planning, estate documents, and liability policies. Simple examples include changing beneficiaries on insurance or retirement accounts, and reallocating a portfolio to reflect a single risk profile instead of two.

Wealth management after divorce isn't just about reestablishing your asset allocation — it's about analyzing your current living situation in light of your new life. Confusion often leads to paralysis; guidance helps clarify the path forward.
