Prenuptial Agreements — Why the Long Face?

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

Prenuptial agreements can be a helpful tool to prepare people for a life-changing event — divorce, or happily staying married. Don't depend on the law to protect you 15, 20, or 30 years from now; the law is open to interpretation and changes with time.

Prenups can make our lives better: protection from alimony-law changes, clear property settlement terms, well-established marriage terms, and a solid understanding of the other person's financial situation. Attorneys often advise each party to get independent counsel regarding a prenup — that way, both sides have time to consider the terms.

Prenuptial negotiation is a fantastic way to understand the finances of the marriage you're entering. If you are planning on marrying, don't consider a prenuptial agreement your enemy.
