$99/Hour Mediation Service Aims to Serve Families

For parties seeking an alternative route for divorce, mediation is the best option. However, the high price tag associated with mediation can create a roadblock. I recently had the opportunity to meet with Lilliana Real, a Mediator I strongly recommend to my clients and colleagues, to discuss why she is launching this service.

Q: Can you tell me a little about yourself? I am a family attorney for the past 9 years and have been a Supreme Court Certified Family Mediator for the past 5 years, since 2009. I have been practicing law for 19 years.

Q: What exciting news do you have for this year? "Starting in 2015, I am offering mediations for $99 per hour due to what I believe is an overwhelming need for affordable mediation."

Q: What factors led you to make this decision? "There are many family law cases where the parties are not willing or able to pay traditional mediation fees of $200–$450 per hour."
