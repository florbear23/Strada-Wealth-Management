Navigating Retirement: A Comprehensive Guide to Investments and Health Care

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

As you move into your retirement years, it's crucial to refine both your investment and healthcare strategies.

Investment strategies for retirement

Transition your investment strategy from wealth accumulation to wealth protection — reducing exposure to high-volatility investments and increasing allocation to fixed-income products like bonds and annuities. A common rule of thumb is subtracting your age from 110 to estimate the percentage of your portfolio to invest in stocks. Employ the 4% rule for withdrawals and review your portfolio annually.

Understanding and managing Medicare

Part A covers hospital stays; Part B covers outpatient care; Part D covers prescription drugs. Medigap plans cover expenses like co-pays and deductibles that Medicare doesn't. Medicare Advantage plans often add dental and vision benefits but may have limited networks.

Conclusion

Whether it's refining your investment portfolio or navigating Medicare, proactive, regularly-reviewed planning is key. Consider consulting with financial and health insurance professionals to tailor your plans.
