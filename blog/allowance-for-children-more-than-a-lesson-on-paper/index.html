Allowance for Children: More Than a Lesson on Paper

Blog

Migrated from the live site's blog archive.

I thought I had a theory about allowance, until I had to put it into motion with my own nine-year-old son — and discovered my husband had a very different one.

My philosophy: allowance shouldn't be paid for normal chores, only for work above the basics. My husband's philosophy: pay for basic chores too. We eventually landed on a creative weekly list — writing a sentence in Italian, one in Spanish, solving a math problem with Papa, reading a book to a sibling — and settled the amount after a parenting specialist's guidance.

Even as a money manager, I really have to think through the process and consider the family's needs. Everyone has different ideas, but in your own financial planning too, it's important to walk through the options — no idea is a bad idea.
