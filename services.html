02 Divorce & Marital Matters Texas-specific

02

Work together and control how you rebuild.

Reach an agreement without going to court through collaborative divorce. You and your spouse work with your own respective lawyers and other experts to resolve issues like child custody and finances — through meetings with both spouses, their lawyers, financial neutrals and mental health process facilitators.

  • Divorce financial planning
  • Collaborative law financial neutral
  • Co-mediation with Board Certified family law attorneys
  • Asset tracing and characterization
  • QDRO coordination
  • Prenuptial and postnuptial agreement review
  • Property settlement review
  • Post-divorce financial rebuilding

The collaborative process focuses on open communication, transparency, cooperation, and less conflict and stress.

03 Professional Partners For counsel

03

Financial clarity for complex cases.

Attorneys are often required to guide clients through financial decisions with long-term consequences — without clear visibility into the outcomes. We provide structured financial analysis in divorce and complex family matters so decisions are made with clarity, not assumption.

  • Asset division modeling
  • Cash flow and support analysis
  • Tax-aware settlement scenarios
  • Long-term financial projections
  • Forensic financial review and tracing
  • Scenario modeling to support negotiation strategy
  • Expert witness testimony

We work as a financial neutral in collaborative matters, as advisor to one party, or as a financial expert consultant supporting case strategy — with defined scope, efficient turnaround, and respect for legal strategy and professional boundaries.

04 When to bring us in For attorneys

Most effective
when engaged early.

Financial clarity can improve negotiation efficiency and help clients understand long-term outcomes before decisions become permanent.

  1. 01

    Before mediation or settlement

    Engaged prior to mediation or settlement discussions, so positions are built on analysis rather than assumption.

  2. 02

    Evaluating multiple scenarios

    When several settlement structures are on the table and the long-term differences between them aren't obvious.

  3. 03

    Complex assets or income

    Business ownership or professional practices, equity compensation or deferred income, high-income or dual-career households.

  4. 04

    Forward-looking modeling

    When clients need help understanding long-term outcomes rather than a snapshot of today.

05 PlanThruDivorce™ Structured process

Evaluate decisions
before they become permanent.

A structured process designed to help clients — and their advisors — work through financial decisions in order.

  1. 01

    Organizing financial data

    Assets, liabilities, income sources and documentation gathered into one accurate picture.

  2. 02

    Modeling settlement scenarios

    Each structure modeled out so its long-term shape is visible before it is agreed to.

  3. 03

    Evaluating trade-offs

    What each option costs and what it protects, set side by side.

  4. 04

    Understanding tax and income decisions

    Tax, charitable and income implications assessed against each settlement scenario.

  5. 05

    Aligning with long-term outcomes

    Decisions checked against the life the plan is meant to support.
