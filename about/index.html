AboutAustin, Texas

Money gets complicated
when life gets complicated.

The practice

Jennifer Failla built Strada Wealth Management around that reality — a family planning firm that values engagement, collaboration, and accountability with its clients.

01Principal Jennifer Failla

Jennifer
Failla

CFP® · CDFA® · CRPC® · MAFF® — Principal

Based in Austin, Texas, Jennifer works with families navigating life's most financially complex moments — divorce, retirement, the loss of a spouse, a major career change. Where other planners see complexity, she sees her specialty.

Her work spans comprehensive financial planning, investment management, tax and retirement strategy, estate coordination, and elder care support. Her divorce practice is among her most distinctive: from prenuptial agreements and property settlement reviews to co-mediation and post-divorce financial rebuilding, she guides clients through the full arc of the process.

02Credentials What the letters mean

CFP®

Certified Financial Planner™

A professional with certification in financial planning — beneficial for crafting a comprehensive plan that aligns with your goals and adjusts as your life changes.

CDFA®

Certified Divorce Financial Analyst

A professional skilled in the financial aspects of divorce — the long-term effects of proposed settlements, tax implications, and asset division.

CRPC®

Chartered Retirement Planning Counselor℠

Focused training in retirement income planning and the transition into retirement.

MAFF®

Master Analyst in Financial Forensics

Training in financial forensics — tracing, valuing, and analyzing assets in complex or contested financial matters.

Also

Certified mediator, collaborative divorce professional, and FINRA Dispute Resolution Arbitrator

Roles that let Jennifer serve as a financial neutral in collaborative matters and support co-mediation alongside Board Certified family law attorneys.

03Philosophy Vision
Understanding first

The goal has never been to manage money for people — it's to make sure they understand it.

Jennifer believes every client deserves the knowledge and clarity to make their own informed decisions, not just a plan handed to them. That conviction shapes everything: how she communicates, how she structures her advice, and why she built a firm where clients leave every conversation feeling more capable, not more dependent.
