02

Registration Status

Conflict — Needs Counsel Before Anything Publishes

The current live site states: "Strada Wealth Management is registered as an investment adviser with the state of Florida and only transacts business in states where it is properly registered, or is excluded or exempted from registration requirements." — repeated identically in the main disclosure, the Facebook disclosure, and the LinkedIn disclosure.

That is the existing, published language, quoted exactly. It has not been changed here. But it doesn't match the operating picture this project has otherwise worked from — a firm based in Austin, Texas, with prior scoping referencing Texas State Securities Board audits. This needs to be resolved against the current Form ADV before this section is finalized: is Florida still accurate, has registration moved to Texas, or is the firm registered in both? This is exactly the kind of "outdated or conflicting registration information" this audit was asked to catch — it's flagged, not resolved, here.

Frozen — Existing Copy

"Registration as an investment adviser does not constitute an endorsement of the firm by securities regulators nor does it indicate that the adviser has attained a particular level of skill or ability."
